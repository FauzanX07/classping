Support

Found a bug, have an idea, or need a hand? We read every message.

From the app

Press Send feedback at the bottom of the sidebar, then choose Feedback or Report a bug. It tells us which version you have, so we can help faster.

By email

Write to classpingsupport@gmail.com. For a privacy question, or to have your messages deleted, we reply within 30 days.

Common questions

Is Class Ping free?

Yes. It is free, with no ads and no tracking.

Does Class Ping see my Google password?

No. With Sign in with Google, the default, you approve Class Ping on Google's own screen and never type your password into it. With Full features, you type it on Google's own sign-in page in Chrome, and it goes only to Google: Class Ping never reads, saves or sends it.

What is Full features?

An optional way to connect, chosen in Settings. Google's official sign-in cannot show comments or open attachments, so Full features signs in to Classroom in Chrome instead. That adds reading and writing comments, attachments inside the app, and searching the words in photos and PDFs. Everything still stays on your PC, and you can switch back at any time.

Is Class Ping made by Google?

No. Class Ping is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Google. It works with your Google Classroom account.

What do I need to run it?

Windows 10 or 11, Google Chrome, and a Google Classroom account.

Do I need an AI key?

No. Posts are sorted on your PC without one. An AI is optional: if you add your own key, it writes a short “what to do” line for each post and can translate posts.

Does Class Ping change anything in my Classroom?

No. It only reads what you can already see. The one exception is a comment you write yourself, which it posts for you.

How do I delete what Class Ping keeps?

Everything stays on your PC. Uninstall Class Ping to remove it; the privacy policy lists every step.

I am under 13. Can I send feedback?

Please ask a parent or teacher first, and leave your name and email out. Parents can ask us to delete any message.
