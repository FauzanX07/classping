A to-do list for Google Classroom, with a notification when a teacher posts.

Class Ping reads the classes you are in, sorts every post into Homework, Classwork and the other sections, shows each due date, and tells you within seconds when something new appears.

Download for Windows Use it in your browser

Free for Windows 10 and 11, with no ads and no tracking. What Class Ping reads stays on your PC.

What it does

  • Notices a new post in seconds. You get a Windows notification and a short chime. The web version does the same, but only while its page is open.
  • Puts homework first. The To-Do list holds only homework, ordered by due date. Announcements, class materials and notes wait in their own sections.
  • Shows the deadline on the post. The due date and time are printed under the title in their own colour, so a deadline is easy to spot.
  • Lets you tick things off. A done post leaves the list; Show done brings it back.
  • Learns where you file things. Sorting happens on your PC. Move a post to another section and Class Ping remembers.
  • Searches everything at once. Titles, text, class names, teachers' names and attachment names. With Full features, the words inside photos and PDFs too.
  • Opens attachments inside the app. Photos, PDFs and videos open in Class Ping. This needs Full features.
  • Reads and writes comments. With bold, italics and lists, without opening the browser. Also Full features.
  • Keeps checking from the tray. Close the window and it carries on. It can start with Windows if you want it to.
  • Uses AI only if you add a key. Off by default. With your own key it can write a one-line "what to do" for each post and translate posts into English.
  • Runs in a browser too New Sign in with Google on a phone, Chromebook or Mac. Nothing to install.
  • Has seven looks. Light, Graphite, Evergreen, Parchment, Chalkboard, Midnight Ink and Ebony, or it follows your device.

How signing in works

There are two ways to connect. You pick one the first time you open Class Ping, and you can switch at any time in Settings.

Default

Sign in with Google

Google's own approval screen opens in your browser, and you allow Class Ping to read your Classroom. You never type your password into Class Ping.

  • Your classes, posts, assignments and materials
  • Due dates, the to-do list, sorting and search
  • A ping when something new is posted
  • Attachments open in your browser
Optional

Full features

Sign in to Classroom on Google's own sign-in page, in a Chrome window Class Ping opens. Your email and password go only to Google: Class Ping never reads, saves or sends them, and takes nothing else from your account.

  • Everything in Sign in with Google
  • Read and write comments
  • Attachments open inside Class Ping
  • Search the words inside photos and PDFs

Either way, there is no Class Ping account and no Class Ping server. Nothing about your classes is sent to us. See where your password goes.

What it uses, and why

Your classesTo know which classes to check, and to show each post with its class.
Posts, assignments, questions and materialsThe whole point of the app: to show them to you, sort them, show their due dates, and notify you when a new one appears.
AttachmentsTo open them inside the app, and to read the words in photos and PDFs so you can search them. This happens on your PC.
Names and pictures of teachers and classmatesTo show who posted or commented, the way Classroom does.
CommentsTo show a post's comments, and to post a comment when you write one.

All of it is kept only on your PC. Class Ping has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells or shares your data. If you turn on the optional AI, the text and photos of each post are sent to the AI company you chose. The privacy policy has every detail.

Under 13, parents and schools

Class Ping is made for students, and some of them are under 13. It has no accounts and does not collect anything about you: what it reads stays on your PC. The only thing that can ever reach us is a message you choose to send from the feedback window. If you are under 13, ask a parent or teacher before you send one, and leave your name and email out. Parents can ask us to delete anything we have: see Children in the privacy policy.

Class Ping reads a school account, so your school's own rules for that account still apply.
