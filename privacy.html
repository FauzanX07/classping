Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This page explains what Class Ping keeps, what it sends, who it goes to, how it is protected, and what you can do about it. It covers the Class Ping app for Windows and this website.

In short
  • Class Ping reads your Google Classroom on your PC and keeps what it reads on your PC. There is no Class Ping account and no Class Ping server holding your classes.
  • Class Ping never sees, types or stores your Google password.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • Two things can leave your PC, and only if you choose them: posts sent to an AI company (off unless you turn it on), and feedback you send us.
  • Under 13? Class Ping collects nothing about you. Ask a parent or teacher before sending us feedback, and leave your name and email out.

1. Who we are

Class Ping is a free Windows app, with a web version at classping.wazeem.com/app, made by a small independent team. "We" and "us" in this policy mean the Class Ping team. You can reach us at classpingsupport@gmail.com for any question, or to ask us to show you or delete anything we hold.

Class Ping is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Google. It works with your Google Classroom account.

2. What stays on your PC

To do its job, Class Ping keeps the following on your PC. None of it is sent to us.

Your Classroom contentClass names; posts, assignments, questions and materials, with their text, due dates and comments; the attachments (photos, PDFs, documents and other files); and the names and profile pictures of the teachers and classmates who appear in them.
Words read from attachmentsText read out of photos and PDFs by Windows' own text recognition, on your PC, so you can search it.
Your choicesSettings, which posts you moved to another section or marked done, and a note of which posts Class Ping has already told you about.
AI keys, if you add anyStored encrypted with Windows' own per-user protection (DPAPI), so only your Windows account on this PC can read them.
Your Google sign-inWith Sign in with Google: the permission Google gives Class Ping, encrypted with Windows' own protection. With Full features: a "stay signed in" record kept by Google Chrome, in a folder used only by Class Ping. Either way, Class Ping never sees your password.
An activity logA record of what the app did, used to find problems. It holds no passwords, but it can contain class and post titles. Old entries are removed automatically.
Unsent feedbackIf you send feedback while offline, the message waits on your PC and is sent when you are back online. It is deleted after 14 days if it still cannot be sent.

In the Microsoft Store version, downloaded attachments are in the folder Documents\Class Ping\Attachments. Everything else is in the app's own storage on your PC.

3. What leaves your PC

Only these, and nothing else:

To Google, to show you your Classroom

Class Ping asks Google for your Classroom, in the way described in section 4, and loads profile pictures from Google. With Full features, it also downloads your attachments and, when you write a comment in Class Ping, posts that comment to Classroom for you. For a YouTube attachment it loads the video's preview picture from YouTube. Google's privacy policy applies to these.

To an AI company, only if you turn it on

AI is off by default: posts are sorted on your PC. If you choose an AI service (Groq, Google Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic Claude) and add your own key, then for each post Class Ping sends that company: the class name, the post's title, its text, its dates, the names of its attachments, and its photos. It uses the answer to write a short "what to do" line, to suggest a section, and to translate posts. A post's text can mention people by name. Class Ping does not send the teacher's name, the comments, or your email address. That company's own privacy policy applies to what it receives. If you choose Ollama, the post goes to Ollama running on your own PC and nowhere else. You can turn AI off, and delete your keys, at any time in Settings.

Feedback you choose to send

Only when you press Send in the feedback window, Class Ping sends us: the name you typed, the email address you typed (optional), your message, the versions of Class Ping and Windows, how posts are being sorted, and, only if you leave the box ticked, recent lines of the activity log with email addresses and web links removed first. It reaches our support inbox. We use it only to read your message, reply to you, and fix problems.

4. How Class Ping connects to Google

You choose one of two ways the first time you open Class Ping, and you can switch at any time in Settings.

Sign in with Google (the default)

You approve Class Ping on Google's own screen, using Google's official Classroom interface. Class Ping asks Google only for read-only access to:

  • your list of classes;
  • the announcements, assignments, questions and materials in those classes, with their due dates and attachments' names and links;
  • the names of the people who post in your classes, to show who posted each item;
  • your own email address, to show which Google account is connected.

What Class Ping does with that data is exactly what this policy describes for the rest of the app: it is shown to you, sorted and searched on your PC, and used to notify you of new posts. It is not sent to us. It is sent to an AI company only if you have turned AI on, as described in section 3. The sign-in permission Google gives Class Ping is kept encrypted on your PC. No person at Class Ping reads your Google data.

Class Ping's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We never sell Google user data, never use it for advertising, and never use it to decide anyone's credit or lending.

You can remove Class Ping's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete what it saved by uninstalling it (see Your controls). If we ever want to use Google data in a new way, we will tell you in the app and ask first.

Full features (optional)

If you turn on Full features in Settings, Class Ping opens Google Classroom in Google Chrome on your PC, and you sign in on Google's own sign-in page. Your email and password go only to Google: Class Ping never reads, saves or sends them. Class Ping then reads the Classroom pages you can already see, downloads their attachments to your PC, and posts a comment only when you write one. Everything it reads stays on your PC, exactly as described above, and the same promises apply: no ads, no selling, no sharing. Turning Full features off in Settings stops it.

5. Class Ping on the web

The web version at classping.wazeem.com/app works inside your browser. It has no Class Ping server: your Classroom is read straight from Google into your browser, and we never see it.

  • Signing in. You approve Class Ping on Google's own screen, for the same read-only permissions as in section 4. Google gives the page an access token that lasts about an hour. It is kept in your browser tab's session storage, is never sent to us, and is forgotten when you close the tab or sign out. The page never sees your password.
  • What it keeps, in your browser only. Your classes and posts, which posts you marked done or moved, a note of which posts you have seen, your theme and notification choices, and the email address of the account you used (to pre-select it next time). This is kept in your browser's own storage (IndexedDB and local storage) on your device.
  • What leaves your browser. Requests to Google to sign you in and read your Classroom, Google's sign-in script from accounts.google.com, and YouTube's preview pictures when a post has a video. There is no AI in the web version, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Hosting. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log the address and requests of people who load the pages. We do not receive or use those logs, and GitHub never receives your Google data.
  • Notifications. Only if you turn them on. Your browser shows them while the page is open; nothing is sent to a server.
  • Feedback. The "Send feedback" link opens your own email app. Nothing is sent unless you send it.
  • Erasing it. In the web version's Settings, "Sign out and erase" gives Google's permission back and deletes everything it saved in your browser. Clearing the site's data in your browser does the same. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The same promises apply as in section 4: Class Ping's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We never sell Google user data, never use it for advertising, and never use it to decide anyone's credit or lending. Section 10 on children and section 11 on schools apply to the web version as well.

6. Who it is shared with

  • Google: to load your Classroom, as above. Our support inbox is also a Google (Gmail) account, so feedback you send is stored by Google for us.
  • The AI company you chose, only if you turned AI on.
  • Nobody else. We have no advertisers, analytics companies, data brokers or other partners. We would share information only if the law required it.

7. How it is protected

  • Class Ping talks to the internet only over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.
  • Saved AI keys, and a Google sign-in permission if you use one, are encrypted on disk with Windows' own protection.
  • Everything else on your PC is protected by your Windows account, so keep your PC locked when you step away.
  • Feedback is protected by the security of our Google account.

8. How long it is kept

  • In your browser (web version): until you press "Sign out and erase" or clear the site's data in your browser. The Google access token is forgotten when you close the tab.
  • On your PC: until you delete it or uninstall Class Ping. You can choose to keep downloaded attachments for only the last 7 days.
  • Feedback: only as long as we need to reply and fix the problem, and never more than one year. Ask us and we will delete it sooner.

9. Your controls

  • See it: everything Class Ping keeps is shown in the app, and the files are in the folders named in section 2. To see the feedback we hold from you, email us.
  • Turn AI off and delete keys: Settings > Sorting and AI.
  • Keep fewer files: Settings > Storage, to keep attachments for only the last 7 days.
  • Delete everything (web version): Settings > Sign out and erase.
  • Delete everything: uninstall Class Ping. The Microsoft Store version removes its data when it is uninstalled. Then delete the folder Documents\Class Ping to remove downloaded attachments.
  • Delete feedback: email classpingsupport@gmail.com.

10. Children

Class Ping is made for students, and some of them are under 13. We take that seriously.

  • We do not collect personal information from children. What Class Ping reads from Classroom stays on the child's own PC and is never sent to us.
  • There are no accounts, no chat with strangers, no ads, and nothing a child can make public through Class Ping.
  • The only way information can reach us is the optional feedback form. It tells anyone under 13 to ask a parent or teacher first, and to leave their name and email out.
  • If we learn that feedback came from a child under 13, we use any contact details in it only to reply once to that message, and then delete the message and the contact details. We never use them for anything else or share them.
  • AI is off by default. A parent can keep it off, so posts never leave the PC.
  • Parents can ask us at any time to show them, or delete, anything we hold from their child, and can tell us not to collect anything more. Email classpingsupport@gmail.com.

11. Schools

Class Ping reads a school Google account, so your school's own rules for that account still apply. Class Ping only shows a student what they can already see in Classroom. It does not change anything in Classroom, except posting a comment when the student writes one.

12. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop using it. Since almost everything stays on your own PC, in practice this means the feedback you sent us. Email us and we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and the new version is published here. If a change affects how your information is used, the app will tell you, and will ask you first where the law or Google's rules require it.
